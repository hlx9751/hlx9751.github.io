**Gua Xiang (卦·象)** is an electronic music work that combines acousmatic composition and interaction. Its conceptual framework is derived from the *I Ching*, drawing from the judgments of hexagrams and lines, as well as related documentary records and oral traditions.

Although the initial design used probability and calculation to randomly select hexagrams, the project gradually shifted toward a more embodied approach. By learning and memorizing divination methods and the imagery of all hexagrams, the work transformed from a system of randomness into a performance shaped by interpretation, memory, and a sense of fatalism.

The interactive music section unfolds as an infinite loop of divination and hexagram interpretation. In each cycle, the performer casts a hexagram with copper coins or palm techniques, transmits the hexagram image to the computer, and triggers sound processing together with CGI-based visual particle effects. The resulting work connects symbolic ritual, algorithmic translation, and live audiovisual performance.

**Materials:** LED screen, custom sliders, Launchpad  
**Technique:** Audio analysis, computer-generated imagery (CGI), particle systems  
**Keywords:** Max/MSP, TouchDesigner, interactive music
