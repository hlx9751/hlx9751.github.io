**Lightning Strike Wood** is a natural phenomenon, as well a quantum phenomenon, and also famous topic in Taoist. it undergoes transient interactions with natural lightning plasma. At the level of wood’s microscopic molecules, lattice defects, electron spins and local quantum entanglement, an irreversible quantum state imprinting and coherent state preservation take place. When crafted into a lightning-struck wood sword, its stable microscopic quantum field and spin-ordered structure can form weak coupling resonance with the human body’s bioelectromagnetic field and neural quantum coherent states. This gives rise to the bodily sensations and effects that ancient people interpreted as "warding off evil spirits, suppressing malevolent forces, gathering vital energy, and calming the mind".

A 3D-printed wood-sword-shaped data-driven instrument controller was developed, integrating a heartbeat sensor, LED light strips, a gyroscope, and buttons to control audiovisual interaction while sensing and synchronizing the performer’s heartbeat signal. It transmits data wirelessly; after being struck by lightning, the generated quantum particles enter an entangled state and dwell within the blade, transforming it into a magical sword that synchronizes with the wielder’s heartbeat.
