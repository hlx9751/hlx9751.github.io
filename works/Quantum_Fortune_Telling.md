**Quantum Fortune Telling** explores conceptual resonances between Taoism and quantum theory by linking quantum output, bodily gesture, and audiovisual response through a performative feedback loop in which the observer is not external to the event.

Powered by real quantum hardware accessed through OSC-Qasm, the work’s generative framework utilises a three-qubit circuit with Hadamard gates to produce eight discrete quantum states, each mapped to one of the eight trigrams of the I Ching.

A cohesive interactive architecture built in Max/MSP facilitates bidirectional MIDI and OSC data transmission across three functional pipelines: OSC-Qasm for quantum data retrieval, Ableton Live for audio synthesis, and cables.gl for real-time visual rendering.

**Keywords:** Quantum music, Interactive multimedia performance, Taoism, I Ching
